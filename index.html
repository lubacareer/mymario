Field guide

Reach the brass bell at the far end of the sky islands. Either courier can make every jump.

Move & jump
Use ← → or A/D to move. Press Space, ↑, or W to jump. Hold for height, tap for a short hop. On touchscreens, hold an arrow while tapping Jump.
Stamps & beetles
Collect up to 12 winged stamps. Land on a puffbeetle from above to bounce. Bumping its side or falling costs one life.
The windmill
Pass the windmill to set your checkpoint. Collected stamps stay in your bag after a fall. Three lost lives end the delivery.
