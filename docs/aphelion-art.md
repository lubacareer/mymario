# Aphelion Run artwork

The built-in ImageGen tool generated three original panoramas at 1672 × 941. The game loads the WebP for the selected course from `public/art/aphelion/`. The canvas renderer draws the ship, landing shadow, track panels, barriers and markings from world coordinates.

## Generation prompts

Each course prompt follows this shared direction.

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one original ultra-wide 16:9 panoramic background painting for a premium retro-futurist spaceflight arcade game, as richly colored cinematic key art on a vintage science fiction paperback cover. Refined airbrushed gouache gradients, subtle fine printed grain and restrained engraved details, beautifully composed. It will sit BEHIND the moving 3D race track. Side to side panoramic scene, landscape orientation. Horizon at about 45% from the top with empty foggy central vanishing point. The lower half is tranquil shadowed void with distant softly layered terrain which reads as far below the racetrack. Deep contrast in outer frame, slightly softer distant center. Frame no road, track, bridge, spaceship, UI, writing, typography, border or watermark.

### dawn-array

Dawn Array: an immense striped amber and ivory planet with thin angled rings occupies the upper-left third, emerging from a persimmon sunset; a second tiny ivory moon, sparse stars against the deep ultramarine and dusty lavender upper atmosphere. Warm golden light flooding the horizon, far serrated rust-colored mesas, silhouettes of distant elegantly cantilevered solar array antennae on the extreme right ridgeline, wisps of purple desert mist. A feeling of anticipation; painterly retrospace frontier.

### iceglass-relay

Iceglass Relay: the dark face of an icy moon is wrapped in a luminous thin turquoise crescent in the upper right third, a faint moon fragment beyond, brilliant emerald aurora ribbons arcing through midnight indigo starfields. A faraway amphitheatre of blue crystalline spires on the far left and right ridgelines, pale mint sunlit edges, suspended blue fog, glacial valleys seen from far above. A cold quiet world, not cyberpunk; evocative tonal detail.

### ember-foundry

Ember Foundry: a bright corona around a small eclipsed vermilion sun in the upper left third, a broad distant arc of drifting ash and asteroid fragments high across a carmine and plum sky. Monumental angular volcanic rock spires rise on the far right and smaller silhouettes at left, faint orange fissures, burgundy mist over a distant magenta caldera. Dramatic molten atmosphere balanced by deep violet shadows, sparse crisp stars, distant metallic glints.
